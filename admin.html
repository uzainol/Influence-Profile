■ FACILITATOR PROMPTS FOR THE DEBRIEF

STRENGTHS AND DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

  • Where in your work have you seen this strength help you?
  • When might this same approach be less helpful, even with good intentions?
  • What situation in the next seven days could be a safe place to try your development lever?
  • What is one small behaviour you can change, rather than a whole personality trait?

AFTER THEY TRY IT

  • What did the other person do or say?
  • What did you learn?
  • What might you adapt next time?

TONE

For Hard levers, avoid character judgments. Discuss context, consent, proportionality and the effect on trust.

Consistency of 80% or above means the profile can be treated as reliable. Below 80% (marked “explore” in the table) is not a problem: probe further. Ask which pairs felt equally true and how their approach changes with different people or situations before drawing conclusions.
